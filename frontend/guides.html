GUIDE 01 / THE CANVAS

Draw & animate

  1. Open the blank canvas and choose a grid size from the top bar.
  2. Choose a colour on the right. Use Pencil to draw, Eraser to remove pixels, Fill to colour an area, or Picker to reuse a colour.
  3. Add or duplicate frames below the canvas to make an animation. Turn on Onion skin to see earlier frames faintly while drawing.
  4. Use Undo and Redo for changes, or Replay to watch how your local artwork was built.

Tip: choose your grid size before you start drawing; add frames when you want to animate.

GUIDE 02 / KEEP YOUR PIXELS

Save your work

  1. Press Save in the editor to save a copy in this browser. Local drawing and saving also work without an account.
  2. To keep a private project in your account, open Save and choose Save to Cloud. Sign in if asked.
  3. Use Open Cloud Project to continue a cloud project. PixelVerse creates a separate local recovery copy of your current canvas before opening it.
  4. Use the export option in Save for a file you can keep or move to another browser.
  5. To remove a cloud save, press the × beside its name in Account and confirm. Your open canvas stays as it is.

Local browser saves stay on that browser. Use cloud saving or export when you want a copy elsewhere.

GUIDE 03 / PIXEL REFERENCE

Draw from an image

  1. Choose Create from image and upload a picture from your device.
  2. Adjust the conversion until the pixel reference looks useful, then press Use as reference.
  3. Draw your own pixels in the editor. Show the reference, trace underneath it, or use Compare with reference to check your work.

The uploaded picture becomes a drawing reference. It does not automatically become your finished artwork. Compare scores the painted reference and extra pixels; empty bands around a fitted image do not earn points.

GUIDE 05 / GALLERY

Share your art

  1. Sign in and press Publish in either editor. Choose a title, description, up to five tags, and visibility.
  2. Open the Gallery to see public artwork. A published post is a fixed copy; later canvas edits do not change it.
  3. Open an artwork to react or comment. You can manage the visibility of your own post later.
  4. Search artwork by title, description, or tag. Open a post and press Save art; your private Saved art tab keeps a list of posts to revisit.
  5. Use artist search to find people by name or username. Open a profile to see public artwork. Click its follower or following count to browse artists and follow them from the list.
  6. Open Following in the Gallery to see public posts from artists you follow. Use View my profile in Account to choose your username, add a bio, and select public artwork for your pixel avatar and featured piece.
  7. Open Activity in the Gallery or the canvas Community menu to see new followers, likes, and comments.

Publishing creates a fixed copy, so you can keep editing your canvas after sharing.

QUICK HELP

Common questions

Do I need an account to draw?

No. You can draw and save locally without signing in. Sign in for cloud projects and publishing.

Why can’t I use cloud features?

Cloud features need an account and a working connection to the PixelVerse server. Your local canvas can still be used when the server is unavailable.

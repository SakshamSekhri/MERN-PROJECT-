PIXELVERSE / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026

PixelVerse is an online pixel art editor and Gallery. This page explains the information the service handles. For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact page.

Information you provide

When you create an account, we store your email address, display name, and a password hash. We do not store your password in plain text. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, and name to identify your account. We do not request access to your Google Drive, contacts, or other Google content.

If you choose to use cloud features, we store your projects, artwork, and selected profile details. Gallery features also store follows, reactions, comments, bookmarks, reports, and related notifications. A Contact message includes the name, email address, category, and text you submit; it is delivered to our support inbox.

What other people can see

Your artist profile can show your display name, username, bio, and public artwork. Artwork you publish as public appears in the Gallery. Unlisted artwork can be viewed by people who have its link. Private artwork and cloud projects require your account. Think carefully before publishing personal information in artwork, descriptions, or comments.

How the information is used

We use account information to sign you in, verify your email, recover access, and protect sessions. We use projects and Gallery information to provide saving, publishing, profiles, and social features. We use Contact messages to respond to your request. The editor also stores some art and preferences in your browser so local work can be recovered; you can clear that browser data through your browser settings.

Service providers and browser resources

Vercel hosts the website and API. MongoDB Atlas stores account and project records. Google handles Google Sign-In when you choose it. Our email provider delivers verification, password reset, and Contact messages. Pages may load fonts and code from Google Fonts and jsDelivr. These providers may process technical request information under their own policies. We do not sell account or artwork data.

Retention and choices

Account and cloud content remain until you remove them or request account deletion. You can edit profile information and remove your published artwork through the app. Session, verification, and reset records expire or are revoked according to their purpose. To request access, correction, or deletion of other account information, use the Contact page. We may need to confirm that you own the account before acting.

Security and changes

We use HTTPS, restricted database access, hashed passwords, and session controls to protect the service. No online service can guarantee perfect security. We may update this policy as the service changes and will update the effective date on this page.
